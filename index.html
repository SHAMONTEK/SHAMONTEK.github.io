For nonprofits

If NSORO hired me, here’s week one.

I’m a NSORO Scholar, so this isn’t a cold pitch — I’ve been on the receiving end of what this org does. Here’s the repair list, in the order that helps families fastest.

  1. Donate in two taps.Sticky donate button, three preset amounts, Apple and Google Pay, no account required. Right now a donor has to want it badly.
  2. One real “get help” page.Plain words, big type, phone number at the very top. A parent in a hurry should not have to hunt.
  3. Photos of actual kids, with consent.Stock photos read as fake to people who know the work. Real faces, signed releases, first names only.
  4. Program pages that answer three questions.Who it’s for, when it meets, what it costs. Every program, no exceptions.
  5. Volunteer and signup without a phone call.A form and a confirmation email. Most people will not call during business hours.
  6. Speed and contrast.Most parents are on cheap phones and aging eyes. Three-second load, AA contrast, readable at 200% zoom.
  7. One number a month that matters.Pick the metric the board actually reads, track it in a sheet, ignore the dashboards nobody opens.

Two-week slices, donate flow first, and a handoff doc so staff can edit without calling me. Case studies live on my phone — ask and I’ll walk you through two.
